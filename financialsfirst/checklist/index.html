Financials First Fast-Track Pre-Flight Checklist

Eight items that must be complete before the 30-day clock starts. We work through all of these with you.

During the discovery phase before your implementation begins, your dedicated ERP Experts project lead will walk your team through each item below. Most of these are things you already have; we help you organise them into the format NetSuite needs. Nothing here is busywork; every item directly affects how cleanly your 30-day go-live runs.

Once all eight items are complete and signed off by both sides, Day 1 is confirmed in writing and the 30-day window opens.

0 of 8 complete

Your progress is reviewed during the initial kick-off call. Once all eight items are confirmed complete, your guarantee activates and the clock starts.

01

Chart of Accounts signed off

Your target account structure agreed and approved by your FD or FC before configuration begins. Changes after this point affect the clock.

Signed off and approved
02

Legacy data extracted

Trial balances, open AP/AR and transactional history exported from Xero, Sage or QuickBooks using our data template. We supply the template.

Data exported and ready
03

Opening balances agreed

A cut-off date confirmed, with a trial balance reconciled to that date. Open AP, AR and agreed master data prior to cut-off are brought in as opening balances. We agree the exact approach with your FD before migration begins.

Cut-off date and balances confirmed
04

Bank and tax details confirmed

Bank accounts, VAT registrations and tax codes documented and verified. HMRC MTD configuration requires your VAT number and filing period confirmed.

Bank and tax details supplied
05

Named project owner

One person on your side with the authority to make decisions and time in their diary. Implementations stall when decisions need escalation. This person must be available throughout the 30 days.

Project owner identified and confirmed
06

Users and roles agreed

A complete list of who needs access, what role they perform in the business, and what approval permissions they hold. This drives the permission structure built into NetSuite.

User list and roles document complete
07

Go-live date in the diary

A committed cut-over date agreed and blocked in your team's calendars. Your project owner and key finance users need to be available for go-live and the first days of live running immediately after.

Go-live date confirmed and blocked
08

Signed Business Requirements Document

The BRD captures the agreed scope, configuration decisions and any specific requirements for your implementation. It is signed off by your project owner. This document locks the scope and formally starts the 30-day clock.

BRD signed off
