What happens if you miss the 30-day deadline?
The final £15,000 payment is waived. You still get a live NetSuite Financials instance, we still deliver the project, and we still honour the 12-month aftercare commitment. The only thing that changes is you do not pay the second half. The deadline is real, and the consequence is ours.
Can we add CRM, inventory, or other modules later?
Absolutely. That is the entire point of Financials First. Once your finance core is stable and your team is confident, we can add modules at your pace. The same team, the same methodology, just scoped and priced as separate phases. No lock-in, no pressure.
Why is the 12-month aftercare mandatory?
Because we have learned, over 350+ projects, that the first year is when things go wrong if they are going to. Month-end closes, audit prep, regulatory changes, new hires who need training. The aftercare is not a revenue stream. It is how we make sure your project is a success story, not a cautionary tale.
What if we need more than 30 hours of support in a month?
Additional hours are available at an agreed rate. In practice, 30 hours is more than enough for a well-configured Financials instance. The first month or two may run closer to the limit, but by month three most clients are using fewer than half their hours. The capacity is there when you need it.
What is the pre-flight checklist, and how long does it take?
The pre-flight checklist is the eight items we need from you to start building. Chart of accounts, bank details, tax info, opening balances, vendor and customer lists, users, sample reports, and NetSuite access. Most organisations can complete it in a week. The 30-day clock starts when the checklist is done, not before.
