The Pre-Flight Checklist

The clock starts when you're ready. Here's what ready means.

Before the 30-day window opens, we run a short discovery phase together. Initial conversations, a review of your current setup and a collaborative working session to work through the items below. We do this with you, not hand it to you and wait.

The checklist is not a barrier — it is the point at which both sides agree everything is in place and we can start with confidence. The 30 days begin the day all eight items are signed off.

Chart of Accounts signed off

Your target account structure agreed and approved by your FD or FC.

Legacy data extracted

Trial balances, open AP/AR and history exported from Xero, Sage or QuickBooks in our template.

Opening balances agreed

A cut-off date confirmed, with a trial balance reconciled to that date. Open AP, AR and agreed master data prior to cut-off are brought in as opening balances. We agree the approach with your FD before migration begins.

Bank and tax details confirmed

Bank accounts, VAT registrations and tax codes documented and verified.

Named project owner

One person on your side with decision authority and time protected in their diary.

Users and roles agreed

Who needs access, what they do, and who approves what.

Go-live date in the diary

A committed cut-over date, with your team available for training and go-live support.

Signed Business Requirements Document

A agreed BRD signed off by your project owner before work begins. This is what locks the scope and starts the clock.

FAQ

The questions finance directors actually ask

£30,000 seems low for a NetSuite implementation. What's the catch?

There isn't one, but there is a reason. Traditional implementations are priced for open-ended scope and human hours. Financials First Fast-Track is a fixed, repeatable scope delivered with AI-accelerated configuration, migration and testing. We've removed the two things that make ERP projects expensive: ambiguity and manual effort.

The trade is clarity. The scope is published on this page. If you need more than the scope, that's a different project, and we'll tell you before you sign anything.

What happens if our scope changes mid-project?

The 30-day guarantee applies to the published scope, agreed at pre-flight. If you ask for additions mid-flight, we'll log them for phase two so the go-live date holds. If a change is genuinely critical to go-live, we'll re-baseline the price, timeline and checklist together, in writing, before any work changes.

How do you deliver an implementation in 30 days?

We use AI to accelerate the parts of an implementation that traditionally eat up the most time: system configuration, data migration, workflow build and testing. This lets our consultants focus entirely on your project, your people and your outcome rather than doing manual technical work.

You never deal with the technology directly. Your single point of contact throughout is your named ERP Experts consultant, who leads the relationship and the result from first call to go-live and beyond.

We have more than one legal entity. Can we still use Financials First Fast-Track?

Yes, with sequencing. Financials First Fast-Track includes one subsidiary on a OneWorld licence, which means additional entities can be added afterwards without re-platforming. Most multi-entity customers go live with their primary entity in 30 days, then roll in the others as a planned phase. If all entities must go live simultaneously, this offer isn't the right shape and we'll scope a standard implementation instead.

Our data is a mess. Does that disqualify us?

No, it just means the clock hasn't started yet. Data readiness is exactly what the pre-flight checklist exists for, and we work alongside you to extract, cleanse and reconcile your legacy data before day one. The 30 days only begin once the checklist, including reconciled opening balances, is signed off. Messy data delays the start, never the guarantee.
